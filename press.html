Short bio

Mammi-Ama is an Ashanti (Akan) American singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist from Amherst, Massachusetts. Classically trained in piano, she also plays African and Indian drums, guitar, clarinet and cello. Since 1998 she has performed at venues including CBGB and Rockwood Music Hall, using music for multi-generational storytelling.
